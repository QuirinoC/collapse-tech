## Listing product (jargon)

Say this out loud before buying anything.

| Phrase | Means | Does **not** mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One bird** | One drone for **exterior stills** and optional **short video** (hero, roof, lot, street context). | A fleet. LiDAR. Aerial survey. Photogrammetry mesh sold as measurement. |
| **Indoor 3D / walkthrough** | Matterport-class or 360 tour so buyers **walk rooms** on a phone. **Separate tool** from the drone. | Drone flying indoors. One device that does house + sky. |
| **Ten agents** | Prospect **10 realtors** (pipeline). | Ten drones, ten cameras, or ten employees. |

**Offer (v1):** listing-ready stills from one bird + optional short exterior clip + optional indoor walkthrough from a dedicated indoor rig. Deliver via a shared album / MLS-friendly files, not a custom app.

**Do not sell:** “survey-grade,” “as-built,” “accurate square footage from the mesh,” or anything a surveyor or appraiser would treat as measurement. Indoor tour is **marketing**, not a property survey.

## Legal and insurance (Washington / Seattle)

### Person vs LLC

| Item | Who |
| --- | --- |
| **FAA Part 107 remote pilot certificate** | **Personal** (Juan). Cannot be issued to Collapse Technologies LLC. |
| **Contracts, invoices, optional drone title** | Collapse Technologies LLC. |
| **Named insured on UAS liability** | LLC (Juan as additional insured / listed pilot as the carrier requires). |
| **WSDOT aircraft registration** (WA) | Can be the LLC once there is an aircraft to register. Confirm current WSDOT UAS rules at registration time; do not skip it. |

Part 107 knowledge test + TSA/application stay on Juan’s name. The LLC does not “hold” the certificate.

### Insurance

- Target: **$1M UAS liability**.
- Ballpark: **~$500–$1,200 / year** (~**$40–$100 / month** equivalent), typically **billed annually**.
- Get **three quotes** (aviation/UAS specialists, not only a homeowners rider). Bind **before** the first paid flight, not after.
- Tell the broker: real estate stills/video, one small UAS, WA operations, no swarm, no LiDAR survey, no indoor drone.

### Airspace and local rules (non-exhaustive)

- **Seattle parks:** treat **park drone flight as banned** unless a written exception exists for that park and date. Do not “quick take” from a park lawn.
- **Sea-Tac / Class B:** **LAANC** (or equivalent authorization) before flying in controlled airspace. No listing is worth a bust.
- Recreational vs commercial: paid listing work is **commercial**. Part 107 applies.
- HOA / private lots: get **property access** from the listing agent or owner. Do not fly a neighbor’s yard as the hero shot without permission.

This section is an operator checklist, not legal advice. Re-read FAR Part 107 and local ordinances before first paid job.

## 90-day success and kill

**Success (in order):**

1. **First paid job** (invoice collected, even if a friends-and-family listing at a real rate).
2. Then: **3 repeat agents** (same agent books again, or three agents each book at least once with intent to reuse).
3. And: **$3,000 revenue in a calendar month** (one month inside the 90 days is enough; do not average three weak months).

**Kill / reallocate:** If there is **no paid job after 8 weeks of real outreach** (not “thinking about Instagram”), **drop sidecar hours into Trust**. Keep the certificate if earned; stop buying kit and stop prospecting until Trust is through the current ship gate.

**Real outreach** means: named agents, messages sent, follow-ups logged, not only a landing page.

## What not to buy yet

Do **not** purchase until the week checklist says so.

- Second drone, cinewhoop, FPV, LiDAR, RTK, survey payload.
- Matterport **Pro3** / enterprise camera before a paid indoor add-on is sold (use a cheaper 360 / Matterport-compatible path or partner for job 1 if needed).
- Gimbal + cinema camera body “for listings.”
- Custom van wrap, studio lighting truck, full-frame mirrorless kit beyond one stills body you already own.
- iPhone Rover BOM / Adafruit cart.
- Ads for listing media before 10 conversations with agents.
- A custom booking app or MLS integration.

**OK to spend early (small):** Part 107 study materials / exam fee, TSA/knowledge-test fees, three insurance quotes (bind only when flying for money is imminent), LAANC app, business cards or a one-page PDF, mileage, a polarizing filter / spare props **after** the airframe is chosen.

**Not in v1 pricing:** twilight interiors, agent headshots, ground-only HDR real-estate photography as a primary trade (unless you already shoot it well).
